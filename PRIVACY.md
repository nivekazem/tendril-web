# 🌿 Tendril Care: Our Privacy Promise
*Understand. Care. Thrive.*

At **Tendril Care**, we believe your botanical passion should be private. Our core philosophy is rooted in one principle: **Your data belongs to you.** This Privacy Promise explains how we protect your information while providing a high-end, intelligent gardening experience.

---

### 1. 🛡️ Privacy by Design: Zero Tracking
We are committed to a tracker-free experience. 
*   **No Analytics:** We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or any other tracking SDKs.
*   **No Crash Reporting:** We do not collect logs about your app usage or device performance.
*   **No Pixels:** We do not use tracking pixels or social media hooks.

### 2. 💾 Storage Sovereignty
You choose how and where your garden history is stored:
*   **Local Storage (Default):** All plant data, photos, and care schedules live exclusively in your device's internal database. If you never sign in, your data never leaves your phone.
*   **Optional Cloud Sync:** If you choose to **"Secure your garden"** via Google Sign-In, your data and photos are encrypted and stored in our private Firebase infrastructure. This allows you to restore your garden across different devices.
*   **Full Offline Backup (ZIP):** We provide a one-tap tool to export your entire garden—including full-resolution photos—into a portable ZIP file for 100% offline control.

### 3. 🤖 Ethical AI & Third-Party Service Integrity
We use industry-leading services to power our "Scientific Engine," ensuring they respect your privacy:
*   **Plant Identification:** Powered by **Pl@ntNet**. Only the specific photo you choose to scan is sent to their non-profit research servers for identification.
*   **AI Health Diagnosis:** Powered by **Google Gemini**. Photos sent for diagnosis are processed ephemerally. We do not store these images to train external AI models.
*   **Climate Data:** Powered by **Open-Meteo**. Only your manually set city or temporary GPS coordinates are used to fetch hyperlocal weather reports.

### 4. 📍 Hyperlocal Location Transparency
If you enable "Use my exact location," your GPS coordinates are used **strictly in memory** to fetch precise climate data for your specific garden. These coordinates are **never stored permanently** on our servers or your device. If you decline location permissions, our manual city search provides full functionality.

### 5. 🚫 No Ads, No Data Sales
**Tendril Care is, and will always be, ad-free.** We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data, photos, or garden history with advertisers or any third-party corporations.

---

### 🗑️ Data Retention & Account Deletion
We respect your right to be forgotten. 
*   **Cloud Users:** You can request the permanent deletion of your synced data and account at any time through the app settings. 
*   **Local Users:** Uninstalling the app immediately removes all locally stored data.
