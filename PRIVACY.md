# 🌿 Tendril Care Privacy Promise
*Understand. Care. Thrive.*

1. **Zero Tracking:** No analytics, no crash reporters, no pixels. We don't know when you open the app.
2. **Local Storage Only:** Your plants, photos, and schedules live entirely on your device. We have no servers.
3. **No Ads:** Free of advertisements, forever.
4. **Ethical APIs:** Identification uses Pl@ntNet (non-profit research); weather uses Open-Meteo (open data). Only the photo you choose and the city you set ever leave your phone.
5. **Optional Exact Location:** If you tap "Use my exact location", your GPS coordinates are used once, in memory, to fetch hyperlocal weather — and are never stored anywhere. Decline it, and the city fallback keeps working.
Uninstall Tendril, and your data leaves with it. Happy growing! 🌱
