🌿 Tendril Care: Our Privacy Promise
Understand. Care. Thrive.
At Tendril Care, we believe your botanical passion is personal. Our philosophy is simple: Your data is yours.
1. Zero Tracking
We do not use analytics, crash reporters, or tracking pixels. We don’t know when you open the app or what plants you own, unless you choose to sync them.
2. Storage: Your Choice of Security
•
Local by Default: By default, your plants, photos, and care schedules live exclusively on your device.
•
Optional Cloud Sync (Google): If you choose to "Secure your garden" by signing in with Google, your garden data and photos are securely stored and encrypted on our Firebase servers, allowing you to recover them on any device.
•
Full ZIP Backup: We provide a 100% offline option to export all your data and photos into a ZIP file that you control.
3. Ethical AI & Data Usage
•
Identification: Powered by Pl@ntNet (non-profit scientific research).
•
AI Diagnosis: When you request a health diagnosis, the photo is processed ephemerally via Google Gemini to identify botanical issues. We do not store these photos to train external models.
•
Weather: Powered by Open-Meteo (open data). Only your specified city or temporary GPS coordinates are used to fetch climate reports.
4. Hyperlocal Location
If you enable "Use my exact location," your GPS coordinates are used only in memory to fetch precise climate data for your garden and are never stored permanently. If declined, the manual city fallback works seamlessly.
5. No Ads & No Third Parties
Tendril Care is, and will always be, ad-free. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or any third-party companies.
Uninstalling the app without using Cloud Sync will permanently delete your local data. If using Cloud Sync, you may request account deletion at any time within the app. Happy growing! 🌱
