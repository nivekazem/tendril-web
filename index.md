# Tendril
**Understand. Care. Thrive.**

Tendril is a privacy-first plant care companion for Android.

- 📷 Identify any plant with a single photo
- 💧 Watering & feeding rhythms with gentle reminders
- 📅 A living calendar of everything you do for your garden
- ⛅ Hyperlocal weather that adapts your plan — always explained, never magic
- 🐾 Honest pet & child safety badges
- 🔒 No accounts. No tracking. No ads. Your plants live on your device.
