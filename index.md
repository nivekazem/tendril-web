# 🌿 Tendril Care
**Understand. Care. Thrive.**

[![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/Release-v1.9.8-1B3022?style=for-the-badge)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tendrilcare.app)
[![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-Android-3DDC84?style=for-the-badge&logo=android)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tendrilcare.app)

Tendril Care is a sophisticated, privacy-centric botanical studio designed for people who believe that understanding nature is the key to a thriving home garden. By combining high-end AI with scientific evapotranspiration models, Tendril provides the most precise care intelligence available for Android.

---

### 🎨 The Botanical Experience

Tendril Care goes beyond simple reminders. It offers a premium **Garden Command Center** that adapts to your life and your climate.

- **🤖 AI-Powered Diagnosis**: Turn your camera into a scientific lens. Instantly identify species and diagnose health issues with actionable recovery plans powered by Gemini AI.
- **⛅ Hyperlocal Intelligence**: Real-time weather data (humidity, UV, temperature) quietly adjusts your watering rhythms. Hot week? Tendril knows your plants need more hydration before you do.
- **📅 Living Garden Legacy**: A beautiful, interactive calendar tracks every watering, feeding, and repotting event, building a lifelong history of your botanical achievements.
- **💍 Vitality Tracking**: Monitor your entire garden’s health through our signature "Vitality Ring" dashboard.
- **🐾 Honest Safety Labels**: Clear, scientifically-backed badges for pet and child safety. No guesswork, just facts.

---

### 🔒 Privacy & Sovereignty: Your Data is Yours

We believe your home is your sanctuary. Tendril Care is built on a **Zero-Tracking** philosophy:

*   **No Analytics**: We don't track when you use the app or what you do.
*   **No Ads**: Free of advertisements, forever.
*   **Data Choice**: Keep your garden 100% local on your device, or choose our **Encrypted Cloud Sync** to protect your history and sync across devices.
*   **ZIP Portability**: Export your entire garden—including full-resolution photos—into a portable ZIP file at any time.
